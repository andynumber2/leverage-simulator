### Exact PROJECT.md Key Decision text (plan 07.1-06 writes this verbatim)

> **PERF-03 D-17 baseline escalation (Phase 7.1 continuation): the `solveIrr` contribution-schedule branch improved 1.83x measured but remains far over budget; escalated with a named remaining lever.** The prior D-17 baseline (CI run 32654061079, `hardwareConcurrency=4`) measured this branch at `measuredMs=7303.90`, `normalizedMs=8961.84`, `sampleCount=1`, roughly 8.96x over the 1000ms budget. 07.1-03 spent two levers against it, both aimed by 07.1-PERF-03-PROFILE.md's measured `npvEvaluationsPerSolve=105.01` and `cashFlowCount=1185`. Lever A replaced the loop's sole termination test (`|npv(mid)| < TOLERANCE`, a dollar-denominated NPV magnitude that was driving the loop to exhaust `MAX_ITERATIONS=100` almost every solve, since a residual below $1e-9 demands far more refinement than an 11-unit rate bracket collapsing to 1e-9 would) with an added rate-space bracket-width test (`hi - lo <= 1e-9`), measured to cut `npvEvaluationsPerSolve` from 105.01 to approximately 36.3, close to 07.1-RESEARCH.md's own naive ~33-34-iteration estimate. Lever B replaced the `CashFlow[]` per-flow object-array representation with `CashFlows`, a pair of parallel `Float64Array` buffers plus a count, eliminating the per-flow heap allocations that dominated at the measured cash-flow count. Combined, the two levers measured a 1.83x wall-clock reduction on a dev sandbox (min-of-3 samples, `hardwareConcurrency=9`: 2003.00ms to 1097.60ms raw), materially short of 07.1-PERF-03-PROFILE.md section 5's 3.14x projection, which assumed nearly all of the branch's cost was `npv` evaluation. That assumption is now known to be wrong: `buildCashFlows`, `runBacktest`, and pool/dispatch overhead do not shrink with fewer `npv` evaluations, so 07.1-PERF-03-PROFILE.md section 5's 3.14x wall-clock figure is superseded by this measured 1.83x on this specific point; its iteration-count finding (the root cause and the ~33-34-iteration prediction) stands confirmed. Scaling this session's own measured normalized reduction by this project's own recorded host-to-baseline ratio (8961.84ms baseline normalized against 3557.88ms local normalized, both pre-change, `hardwareConcurrency=4` vs `hardwareConcurrency=9`, same code) projects the D-17 baseline normalized figure after these two levers at approximately 4871.75ms, with a plus-or-minus 13% band (WINDOWS.md entry 2) of [4238.42, 5505.08]ms, still roughly 4.24x to 5.51x over the 1000ms budget even at the low end of that band. This is a projection, not a measurement; plan 07.1-06's real CI run against the D-17 baseline replaces it with the actual figure. Of D-20's remaining levers, the only one with plausible headroom against a residual multiple-x gap is Newton-Raphson with the existing bisection kept as a fallback (when a derivative step leaves the bracket or fails to converge in a small iteration budget), scoped to the sweep call site only, per 07.1-RESEARCH.md's own Don't-Hand-Roll guidance: Newton converges in roughly 5-10 iterations against bisection's tens for the same tolerance. This reopens D-08, which rejected a two-solver path on the premise that IRR "runs once per parameter change, over a few hundred cash flows, where speed is not the constraint." The sweep call site runs `solveIrr` 10,000 times per sweep, over cash-flow sequences up to roughly 1,185 flows; the premise D-08 was written against does not hold at this call site, which is why a Newton-with-bisection-fallback path needs its own recorded Key Decision rather than a silent edit, not because D-08 was wrong where it was written. **Decision: escalate.** Adopting Newton-with-bisection-fallback is deferred to a future planning cycle (`/gsd-plan-phase 7.1 --gaps` or a later phase), scoped to the sweep call site only, with `solveIrr`'s existing signature and its single-run caller in `src/app/state.ts` untouched, and the existing bisection kept as the correctness-proven fallback (Descartes' rule, exactly one sign change by construction) rather than a from-scratch replacement. **No budget value, threshold, anchor, calibration constant, run-level cap, or grid dimension moved to reach this disposition**: `PERF-03.thresholdMs` stays 1000, `NOMINAL_REFERENCE_MS` stays 40, `BENCH_TOTAL_RUNTIME_CAP_MS` stays 30000, the default grid stays 200x50. **PERF-03 remains FAILED for this branch, not met**, exactly as Phase 7's own D-17 baseline escalation recorded for the headline row.
