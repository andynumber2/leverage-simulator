<objective>
Put the canonical arguments one click from the landing state.

**User story (MVP mode):** **As a** person who wants to point at the strongest version of the
argument rather than construct it, **I want to** click a named scenario and land directly on its
result, **so that** the link I share is a specific reproducible window rather than a claim.

Purpose: SHARE-06 and roadmap criterion 3's "each one click from the landing state and each a
shareable permalink". Plan 08-03 built the data and its proof; this plan builds the surface.

Output: `PresetCard`, `FeaturedPresetRow`, `ScenariosOverlay`, the `applyPreset` write path in
`state.ts`, and a browser test that clicks a preset and checks the resulting permalink.
</objective>
