<objective>
Measure what the two export paths and the DCA preset actually cost the main thread, and record the
figures honestly whether or not they fit.

**User story (MVP mode):** **As a** person exporting a result mid-argument, **I want to** click
Export and have the page stay responsive, **so that** the tool does not visibly stall at the exact
moment I am using it to make a point.

Purpose: roadmap criterion 4 ("the export path stays inside the 50ms main-thread task budget,
measured"), D-05, and the two open unknowns the research explicitly refused to estimate around:
F-05 (no figure exists for the PNG path, which cannot leave the main thread by construction) and
RESEARCH Pitfall 2 (the CSV worker's structured-clone dispatch still runs on the main thread).

Output: a new bench file and browser command producing three real figures, plus a recorded
escalation if any breaches, plus the Safari verification this repo's Chromium-only test matrix
cannot provide.
</objective>
