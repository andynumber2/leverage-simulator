<objective>
Ship the CSV export path: a single run's daily series, with enough columns and enough provenance
that a skeptic can rebuild the recurrence in a spreadsheet and land on the same final value.

**User story (MVP mode):** **As a** skeptic being handed a leveraged-ETF result, **I want to**
download the run's underlying daily series with every input the model consumed, **so that** I can
redo the arithmetic myself instead of taking the app's word for it.

Purpose: SHARE-05 and roadmap criterion 2. This is the requirement that makes the app's core claim
falsifiable by a stranger.

Output: `src/export/csv-columns.ts`, `src/export/csv.worker.ts`, `src/export/csv-preamble.ts`,
`src/export/csv-export.ts`, the wired Export CSV button, and a Node-side test that recomputes the
recurrence from the emitted text.
</objective>
