<objective>
Make every default visible as a default and editable in place (CRED-05), across the whole
parameter surface rather than the two cost fields Phase 4 covered.

**User story (MVP):** **As a** skeptic reading someone else's backtest, **I want to** see which
parameters are still at the tool's shipped defaults and change any of them in place, **so that** I
cannot be told the result depends on assumptions I was never shown.

Purpose: PITFALLS G3 — defaults are a claim. A default entry date is a far bigger claim than a
default expense ratio, and it was the one parameter with no treatment at all.
Output: a defaults registry, two shared components, a new initial-investment control, and the
treatment applied to every defaulted parameter.
</objective>
