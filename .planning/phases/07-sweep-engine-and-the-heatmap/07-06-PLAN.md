<objective>
Give the sweep its two controls and make its state shareable, without inventing a second parameter
model or a second holding-mode control.

Purpose: D-15's mode switch is what fills the single result slot `App.tsx`'s D-21 reserved. D-04's
permalink extension is the project's stated core value, and Phase 8's curated permalinks depend on
it. D-23's metric toggle is what makes METR-06's single-pass computation visible to the user.
Output: two segmented controls, two new permalink keys, and a result column that switches between
one defensible answer and ten thousand of them without losing the reader's position.
</objective>
