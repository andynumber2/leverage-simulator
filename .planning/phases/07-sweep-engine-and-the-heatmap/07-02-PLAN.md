<objective>
Give each of the three swept metrics the colour scale its semantics actually call for, inside the one
module that is already the colour authority.

Purpose: D-25 closes PITFALLS E1 (a sequential quantity must not be given an invented midpoint). It
is also the enabling change for the metric toggle: without a second ramp, switching to max drawdown
would silently paint a pure magnitude through a diverging blue-orange scale.
Output: a parameterized interpolator, a sequential violet ramp, three fixed domains, and per-metric
band boundaries, all under the same measured perceptual and colourblind bars the diverging ramp
already clears.
</objective>

1. **The colour ramp.** `interpolateRamp` was written as THE ramp: it closes over module-level
   `RAMP_STOPS_OKLAB` and takes no stops argument. D-25 introduces a second ramp.
   - **Primary noun:** a ramp interpolator built over an explicit stops array.
   - **Decision: `promote`.** `buildRampInterpolator(stops)` becomes the primary and the existing
     `interpolateRamp` is demoted to one instantiation of it, rather than a second parallel
     interpolator being added alongside a still-primary `interpolateRamp`. Adding alongside would
     have left the diverging ramp structurally privileged, and the next metric that needs a third
     family would face the same refactor again.

   mode.

   - **Decision: `promote`.** `resultMode` becomes the primary and the Phase 4 and 5 single-run stack
     is demoted to one branch of it, which is what the D-21 single result slot was reserved for.
     Plans 07-01 and 07-06 implement this.
