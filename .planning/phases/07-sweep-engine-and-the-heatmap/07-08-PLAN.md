    - A pointer exactly on a cell boundary resolves deterministically to one cell, and the rule is stated in the module header rather than left to floating-point chance.

Add the unit tests for every behavior above to a new block in `tests/heatmap/polygon-fill.test.ts`'s

leverage guide line dashed rather than solid at the clamped edge row, so a clamped position is never
presented as an exact one.

its colour. Field labels are exactly `Entry`, `Leverage`, `Multiple`, `Drawdown`, and `IRR` or
