|----------|-------------|
| Categorical cell flag to rendered pixel | A ruined cell that falls through to the continuous ramp reads as a merely-bad number rather than a total loss |
| Date-derived boundary to rendered rule | A boundary computed from cell values rather than dates would move under a chunk failure and mislabel which columns are short-horizon |
