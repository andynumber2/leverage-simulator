## The four judging criteria

Fixed here so the choice is argued rather than aesthetic, and so a rejection reason has something
concrete to cite. `06-CONTEXT.md` deferred a formal written scorecard as extra process; these four
are the criteria the roadmap's own success criteria already imply, not a new instrument.

1. **Does the breakeven boundary read?** Multiple-of-contributed has a real threshold at 1.0x and
   the diverging centre encodes it. A form where a reader cannot find "did you get your money
   back" fails the question the tool exists to answer (D-13, PITFALLS E1).
2. **Do the two categorical states read as categorically different?** Ruin as hatch and
   incomplete-hold as flat grey must not read as the extreme ends of the continuous scale
   (D-18, D-20, PITFALLS E4 and E5).
3. **Does it survive dark mode and a screenshot crop?** One background-neutral palette, both
   themes, and the caveat inside the screenshot region (D-06, D-15, D-21).
4. **Does the caveat sit where it will actually be read?** A form that wins on field legibility by
   demoting the caveat to a footnote fails success criterion 3 on its own terms (D-21, D-23).

Criterion 4's repaint figures are a PASS or FAIL gate at 16ms per form, never a tiebreaker between
forms: per D-12 and finding F-02, the four figures are taken at four different geometries and are
not comparable to each other.
