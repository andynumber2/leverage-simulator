- **PERF-07 (unclassified, unresolved).** No shape category was proposed for "No main-thread task
  exceeds 50ms during any interaction, verified by long-task measurement. Dragging a slider sustains
  60fps". Two planner assumptions are load-bearing and are stated here rather than buried. First,
  "any interaction" is discharged for this phase by the leverage slider drag, because it is the only
  continuous-input control the phase ships and the only one whose recompute cost scales with the
  parameter; the discrete controls (symbol, dividend mode, holding mode, frequency) fire the same
  coalesced recompute once, so measuring the continuous case bounds the discrete ones. Second,
  "sustains 60fps" is discharged by PERF-07b's 16ms ceiling on the app's own coalesced recompute
  rather than by a raw frame-delta measurement, because a headless CI browser's frame cadence also
  reflects compositor scheduling the app does not control, and D-03 already names that callback as
  what the 16ms row measures. If either reading is wrong, the harness's target changes, not the
  budgets.
</flagged_assumptions>
