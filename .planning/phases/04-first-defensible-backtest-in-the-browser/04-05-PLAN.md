From `src/data/contribution-schedule.ts`: `ContributionFrequency` is the five-member union `none`,
`daily`, `monthly`, `quarterly`, `yearly`, re-exported from `src/data/kernel-inputs.ts`. Import it;
do not re-declare the list in the component, or the closed set can drift from the schedule resolver.

From `src/data/kernel-inputs.ts`, the two thrown messages this plan's explanations render:

- Entry date out of range: names the offending value and the supported range of the resolved series.
- Holding period past the end: names `holdingPeriodBars`, the entry date, the maximum bar count and
  the date the run would have to end on. This is the exact D-10 cross-field case, and its message
  already carries the limiting date the caveat copy needs.

Also from `src/data/kernel-inputs.ts`: `holdingPeriodBars` of `null` means hold to the last
fully-supported bar (D-29), and `0` is in range and means a one-bar run, not a zero-bar run, so the
control's minimum is 0 rather than 1.

From `KernelInputs.meta`: `truncatedForRateCoverage` is already computed and is true when D-29's
rate-coverage truncation shortened the window below what the price series alone would support. The
caveat surface reads it rather than recomputing the comparison.

From `src/validation/cost-parameters.ts`: `GENERIC_3X_EXPENSE_RATIO` and `FINANCING_SPREAD_DEFAULT`
are FRACTIONS; `BacktestRequest.expenseRatioPercent` and `financingSpreadPercent` are PERCENTAGES.
Multiply by 100 exactly once, when seeding the control, and never again (D-09, F-02).
`FINANCING_SPREAD_RANGE` carries the cited `lower` and `upper` bounds the midpoint is derived from,
and `COST_PARAMETERS` carries each entry's `citation` and `confidence` tag.

From plan 04-04: `ParameterColumn`, `SourceCitation`, and `ValidationExplanation`'s single-field
eviction variant already exist and are extended here rather than replaced.
