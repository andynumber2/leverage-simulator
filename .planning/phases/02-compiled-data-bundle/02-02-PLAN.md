### Declared constants (Claude's Discretion under CONTEXT.md, fixed here)

| Constant | Value | Meaning | Why this number |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RATE_CARRY_FORWARD_LIMIT_DAYS` | `4` | Maximum permitted difference in days-since-epoch between two adjacent rate observations before the gap is fatal | D-09's stated driver is Columbus Day and Veterans Day, when the bond market closes and the stock market stays open. A bond-market holiday falling on a Monday puts the previous observation on the Friday and the next on the Tuesday, a difference of 4. A plain weekend is 3. Four therefore admits every legitimate case D-09 names and nothing wider |
| `STALENESS_WARN_DAYS` | `10` | A series whose last date trails the newest date in the bundle by strictly more than this many calendar days emits a warning | A current daily series is at most 4 calendar days behind over a long weekend, so 10 leaves real slack while still catching a refresh that failed weeks ago, which is the operational failure D-12 exists to detect |

### `gap-policy.ts` contract

`applyGapPolicy(series, calendar, exceptions, seams)` returns `GapPolicyResult` carrying the aligned `Float64Array`, the `calendarStartIndex`, and a list of warnings. It throws a single error naming every offending date at once rather than the first, so one compile run tells the operator everything that needs fixing.

Classification, in this order:

1. A date present in the series but absent from the reference calendar is an **extra bar**. Fatal unless an exception matches that exact scope and that exact date (D-10, D-11).
2. A calendar date missing from a series whose `seriesKind` is `price` or `total-return`, and lying strictly inside that series' own first and last dates, is an **interior price gap**. Fatal unless an exception matches (D-09).
3. A calendar date missing from a series whose `seriesKind` is `rate`, lying strictly inside its own range, with an adjacent-observation difference of at most `RATE_CARRY_FORWARD_LIMIT_DAYS`, is **carried forward** and recorded as one `carry-forward` seam (D-09). Beyond the limit it is fatal.
4. Calendar dates before a series' first date or after its last date are its ragged edges, never a gap. Ragged left edges are normal (UPRO 2009, TQQQ 2010, EEM 2003). A ragged right edge produces a staleness warning past the threshold and never an abort (D-12).

`CalendarException` is `{ scope: string, date: string, reason: string }`. `loadCalendarExceptions(rawDir)` returns an empty list when the file is absent, validates every entry against an explicit field allowlist, requires `reason` to be a non-empty non-whitespace string, requires `date` to match `YYYY-MM-DD` exactly, and rejects any entry carrying a range, a wildcard, a glob or an extra key.
</interfaces>
