Extend `src/validation/cost-parameters.ts` with the D-14 derivation. Declare a
`ToleranceMechanism` interface carrying `id`, `appliesTo` (`'tracking-error'` or `'drift'` or
`'both'`), `direction` (`'one-directional'` or `'bidirectional'`), `basisPointsPerYear`,

`CostParameterConfidence`. Declare `TOLERANCE_MECHANISMS` as a readonly array of them.

Price the drift gate (Gate 2, the annualized return difference, which catches cost-model errors)
against at least these five mechanisms, each with its own stated basis: the expense ratio's real
365-day internal accrual versus this model's calendar-day approximation of it; swap dealer spread
variation over time against a single committed constant; fund trading and rebalance costs crossed
on the daily rebalance, which this model does not represent at all; securities-lending revenue the
real fund may earn and the synthetic does not credit; and the inception-era expense-ratio
uncertainty itself, whose magnitude follows directly from whether Task 2 resolved those figures to
`CITED` or left them `ASSUMED`. That last row is load-bearing and must be priced from Task 2's
actual outcome, not from an assumption about it.

Price the precision gate (Gate 1, the annualized standard deviation of daily return differences,
which catches mechanism errors) against its own separate mechanism list, because the drift
mechanisms above do not produce daily dispersion: the fund's intraday rebalance timing versus this
model's close-to-close bars; the fund's NAV-versus-market-close pricing basis against the index's
close; discrete swap reset dates against this model's continuous daily accrual; and the D-29
ragged right edge where the rate series ends one bar before the price series.

Compute `RETURN_DRIFT_TOLERANCE` and `TRACKING_ERROR_TOLERANCE` as the sum of their applicable
mechanisms' `basisPointsPerYear` multiplied by a safety factor of 1.5, and declare that factor as
a named constant whose comment states the reason: the enumerated magnitudes are order-of-magnitude
reasoning rather than measurements, so the derived bound carries a margin for the reasoning itself
being off by half. Compute both tolerances from `TOLERANCE_MECHANISMS` in code rather than writing
either as a literal, so the derivation cannot drift from its own inputs and so a mechanism added
later mechanically widens the bound in the same diff that names it, which is exactly D-15's
revision rule made structural.

derivation was written, and that both figures are revisable only by adding or repricing a named
mechanism in `TOLERANCE_MECHANISMS`, never by editing a tolerance directly.
