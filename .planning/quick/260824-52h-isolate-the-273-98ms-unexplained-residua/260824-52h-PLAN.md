Established during planning, do not re-derive and do not contradict without evidence:

1. Chunk buffers are ALREADY transferred, not copied, in both directions
   (src/sweep/sweep-pool.ts:377 and src/sweep/sweep.worker.ts:283, both Comlink.transfer). An
   ArrayBuffer transfer is O(1) in the buffer's size. This materially weakens candidate (a) as
   the profile stated it. What DOES scale with cell count on that path and must be measured
   rather than assumed: the zero-initialized `new ArrayBuffer(chunkBufferByteLength(cellCount))`
   allocation at sweep-pool.ts:352, the four typed-array `.set()` copies inside the worker's
   runChunk, and the main-thread merge of each returned buffer. Expected small at roughly 160KB
   total across the grid. Measure both directions of that expectation.

2. src/sweep/sweep-pool.ts needs NO edit. Its `workerFactory` option is an existing production
   seam, already used from the bench tree by bench/sweep-progressive.bench.test.ts line 235 to
   construct the real production worker. A factory that shadows the returned worker's
   `postMessage` and registers its own `message` listener observes every chunk dispatch and every
   chunk response at the real pool boundary. Buffer-allocation and merge cost, which happen
   between those two events on the main thread, are bounded above by the observed response-to-
   next-dispatch window and measured separately at the same shape.

3. partitionColumns produces exactly `BASELINE_WORKER_COUNT * CHUNKS_PER_WORKER = 3 * 4 = 12`
   chunks at any column count above 12. At SWEEP_COLS=200 that is 8 chunks of 17 columns and 4
   of 16. At the reduced PROFILE_COLS=12 it is 12 chunks of 1 column. The recorded
   perCellKernelUs=255.42 figure came from the ORIGINAL PROFILE_COLS=24, that is 12 chunks of 2
   columns. The linearity test therefore contrasts span 2 against span 17.

4. Comlink's expose callback returns early on an unknown message `type` (`default: return`), and
   Comlink's main-side per-call listener returns early on any message whose `id` does not match.
   A raw side-channel message in either direction is inert with respect to the runChunk round
   trip. Verify this empirically in Task 1 rather than trusting the reading.

5. This sandbox reports hardwareConcurrency=9 and is NOT the D-17 baseline. Every figure this
   task produces locally is a sandbox figure and must be labelled as one (PERF-11). The
   authoritative D-17 attribution requires a CI run on GitHub Actions ubuntu-latest at
   hardwareConcurrency=4, which .github/workflows/ci.yml triggers on `pull_request` only. This
   task does NOT push, does not merge, does not mark ready, and does not comment on PR #7 or
   PR #8. FINDINGS.md states the CI requirement as an open next action for the user.
